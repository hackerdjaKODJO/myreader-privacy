Last updated: 13 November 2026
Applies to: MyReader for Android, package com.myreader.app (including version 2.11.0)

The short version

  • The developer does not receive or store your documents, reading history, notes, questions or account data during normal app use. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. If you choose to submit an AI answer report, the question/answer excerpt you submit is posted to a public GitHub issue (see sections 7 and 15).
  • Documents and reading data are processed and stored on your device. Android cloud backup is disabled, so the system backup service does not upload MyReader’s reading state to Google Drive.
  • App-initiated network access is used only for optional, user-initiated features: selected-text translation through Google Translate, web references, and voice/translation/Reading AI model downloads.
  • Spoken-sentence translation is always processed on the device. It does not send sentences to Google Translate.
  • Web references are off by default. When you turn them on, your question (and the book title for book-scoped questions) is sent to public search providers and Wikipedia. Book text, indexed passages, selected passages and notes are not sent.
  • Optional AI and voice packs are large downloads. They are not bundled in the APK and are stored in the app’s private storage until you delete them.
  • The release app does not write a diagnostics log. Development/QA builds may keep a local log that is never uploaded automatically.

1. Who we are

MyReader is an independent Android document reader developed by the MyReader project (an individual developer). This policy explains how the app handles information when you read documents, use read-aloud, use the optional Reading AI, translate text, turn on web references, or download optional model packs.

The controller for the processing described here is the MyReader project. A public support email is provided on the Google Play store listing and is the preferred privacy contact. If you cannot use that channel, open an issue at the policy repository without including personal data and ask for a private follow-up channel.

This policy applies to the app itself. It does not apply to websites, search engines, operating-system services or apps you open from MyReader. Those services have their own policies, linked below where relevant.

2. What stays on your device

MyReader is designed around local processing. The following data is stored in the app’s private storage and is not sent to the developer:

  • Documents you open. PDF, DOCX, PPTX, HWP, HWPX, EPUB and TXT files are opened through the Android system file picker or an explicit ACTION_VIEW share grant. The app does not modify the original file. Temporary conversion files (for example, a text fallback PDF or a generated “book view” EPUB) are written to the app’s private cache.
  • Reading state. Last page or position, recent-document titles and URIs, bookmarks, highlights, display theme, brightness, keep-screen-on preference, reading speed, voice settings and similar preferences.
  • Reading AI data. The optional local search index (book text chunks and vectors in a private SQLite database), saved AI notes, generated artifacts and chat memory. These are described in section 7.
  • Optional model packs. Downloaded voice, translation and Reading AI model files in the app’s private storage.
  • Diagnostics. Release builds do not write a log; development/QA logging is described in section 8.

Android cloud backup is disabled for MyReader (android:allowBackup="false"). As a result, the Android system backup service does not upload MyReader’s reading state to Google Drive or transfer it to another device. Uninstalling the app removes its private data.

3. What the developer collects

During normal app use, the developer does not receive or store personal data from MyReader. There is no account system, no developer server, no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no tracking SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. The developer does not sell or rent personal data.

MyReader does make some optional, user-initiated transmissions directly from your device to third-party services, and it offers a user-initiated AI answer report that posts to a public GitHub issue. These are described in sections 4–7. Under Google Play’s Data safety rules, transmitting data off the device can count as “collection” or “sharing” even when the developer never operates a receiving server. This policy describes those transfers plainly rather than claiming that “nothing ever leaves the device.”

The developer does not use your documents, notes or questions to train models. When you choose to send content to a third party (for example, selected text to Google Translate or a question to a search provider), that provider handles the content under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Google Translate for selected text

When you select text in a document and choose Google Translate, MyReader translates the selected text.

  • If the optional TranslateGemma pack is installed: MyReader first tries to translate on the device. If local translation succeeds, no text is sent to Google.
  • If the pack is absent, or local translation fails: MyReader opens an in-app WebView containing Google Translate and passes the selected text as the query, together with source/target language parameters. That is a user-initiated transfer of the selected text to Google.

Only the selected text is sent—not the whole document, the file, your reading history, your notes or your other documents. The app does not store the selection or the translation result beyond an in-memory translation cache. Google’s handling of the text inside the WebView is governed by Google’s privacy policy and the applicable Google terms.

The Google Translate WebView has JavaScript and local storage enabled. Google may store cookies or local storage in the WebView, subject to its own policy and your WebView settings. You can remove this data by clearing MyReader’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

If you do not want this transfer, do not tap Google Translate. You can install the optional TranslateGemma pack to translate selected text locally, or copy the text and use a service you choose.

Spoken-sentence translation (off by default)

The separate Translate spoken sentences setting is off by default. When enabled, MyReader translates each sentence it reads aloud using only on-device processing: the optional TranslateGemma pack first, then the Android on-device translation service. If neither can handle the language pair, no translation is shown. This feature does not contact Google Translate and does not send spoken sentences over the network. The translated line is shown in a translucent subtitle on the reading screen, and the original sentence remains the reading/highlight anchor.

5. Web references (optional, off by default)

Reading AI has an optional Web references setting. It is off by default. When you turn it on and ask a question, MyReader may send the following to public search services and Wikipedia:

  • the text you enter in the question box;
  • for book-scoped questions, the selected book’s title (metadata, up to 120 characters) as additional search context.

MyReader does not send book text, indexed passages, selected passages, saved notes, chat history or files as part of a web-reference query. If you paste book text into the question box yourself, that text is sent as part of your question.

Turning Web references off stops new requests; a request already in flight may finish. The app continues to use only the local index when web references are off.

Links embedded in documents open in your device’s browser (or the app you choose). Those visits are user-initiated and are governed by the browser/site policies, not by MyReader.

6. Voice, translation and Reading AI downloads

MyReader does not bundle the large optional models in the APK. Each pack is downloaded only after you confirm, over HTTPS, and is stored in the app’s private storage. The download request includes standard HTTPS metadata (such as your IP address, user agent and the requested file) but does not include document text, notes, questions or reading history. Model files are checked against pinned SHA-256 values.

System text-to-speech

Read-aloud can use the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. MyReader passes the text to that engine. Some engines can use network voices; in that case the selected text is handled by the engine and its provider under their own privacy policy. MyReader does not upload the text itself. Choosing an on-device voice, or installing the optional Supertonic/Chinese pack, avoids a network voice.

Download hosts and their content-delivery providers may process standard request metadata under their own policies. See Hugging Face’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

7. Reading AI, notes and chat

Reading AI is optional and requires a separate model pack (section 6). When installed, the following happens on the device:

  • Book text is extracted, chunked and stored in a private SQLite index, together with vectors used for search. Extracted text and vectors stay in the app’s private storage.
  • Questions, answers, summaries, explanations and chat are generated by the local model. No document text, index passage, note or chat message is sent to a developer server.
  • Saved notes may include a selected passage, an AI answer, a book title and a timestamp. Notes are stored locally. When the global Use notes as AI context switch is on, notes can be included as local context for your own AI questions; they are not sent to web search.
  • Book chat and general chat keep a small local conversation memory so the thread survives leaving the screen. Long conversations are compacted locally.

Deleting AI data: the in-app AI 데이터 삭제 / Delete AI data action deletes the local search index database, saved AI notes, generated AI artifacts, the AI cache and local chat memory. It does not delete your bookmarks or highlights, and it does not delete installed model packs.

Accuracy: AI-generated answers can be incomplete, outdated or wrong. They are not professional advice. Check important facts against the original document. The app does not publish your AI output automatically; you can share it through Android’s share sheet or submit a public AI answer report when you choose.

Sharing and AI reports: when you share a document, note or diagnostic log, Android opens the system chooser and sends the item only to the app you choose. If you tap Report / 신고 on an AI answer or an AI-generated artifact, the app opens its public GitHub issue form inside an in-app WebView and sends the question, answer, optional comment, app version and Android version so the developer can review it. The app requires an explicit “I understand this report will be public” confirmation before the report button is enabled. Reports are public; do not include personal information or whole documents. MyReader does not operate a separate sharing server.

Model licenses: Gemma 4 E2B is Apache-2.0; EmbeddingGemma and TranslateGemma are under the Gemma Terms of Use; Supertonic 3 is under BigScience OpenRAIL-M. The app’s About → open-source licenses and model-license dialogs contain the notices. Do not describe all models as “open source” without checking the specific license.

8. Diagnostics and local logs

The release app does not create or write a diagnostic log. There is no Diagnostics button in the release app, and WebView remote debugging and page-console logging are disabled.

Only development/QA builds may keep a small rotating local log for troubleshooting. That log is never uploaded automatically. If a QA tester taps Share, Android opens the system chooser and sends the log only to the app they choose. It can be cleared from the same dialog, and uninstalling MyReader removes it.

Do not post a diagnostic log publicly if it contains information you consider private.

MyReader declares the following Android permissions. It does not request broad storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call-log, advertising-ID or billing permissions.

Files are opened through the Storage Access Framework or an explicit ACTION_VIEW grant. MyReader does not request MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and does not scan your storage.

11. Security

App-initiated network requests use HTTPS; Android cleartext traffic is disabled (android:usesCleartextTraffic="false"). Model files are verified against pinned SHA-256 values. Documents and app data are stored in the app’s private storage, and only the main activity is exported; other activities, services and content providers are not public.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe you have found a security issue, please report it through the contact channel below without posting sensitive document contents publicly.

12. Children

MyReader is a general-purpose document reader and is not directed at children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If a child uses the app, a parent or guardian should supervise the use of optional AI, translation, web-reference and model-download features.

13. International users

The developer does not operate a server or maintain a user database. When you use an optional feature, the third-party provider you choose may process the request in a country other than yours, under its own privacy policy and applicable law. If you do not want a transfer to a particular provider, do not use that feature or use an on-device alternative where available.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the developer relies on: (a) consent for optional transfers you explicitly start, including Google Translate, web references, public AI reports and optional model-pack downloads; (b) legitimate interests in keeping the app secure, preventing abuse and responding to support requests; and (c) legal obligations where applicable. You may withdraw consent at any time by disabling the feature or not submitting the report; withdrawal does not affect processing that already occurred.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or a copy of personal data; to object to processing; to withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. MyReader has no account or developer-held user database, so most reading data can be deleted directly on the device with Clear storage or by uninstalling. For a public AI report, edit or delete the GitHub issue or request removal through the issue tracker. To exercise a privacy right, use the support email on the Google Play listing and describe the request without including document contents or other sensitive data.

United States (California and similar states)

The developer does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. MyReader has no advertising or analytics SDK. California residents may request to know, delete, or correct personal information, and may exercise those rights without discrimination. Because the app has no account, requests concerning on-device reading data can usually be completed by clearing app storage or uninstalling; public AI reports can be edited or deleted on GitHub.

Korea

Under the Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information. Reading data stays on the device and can be deleted with Clear storage or by uninstalling. Optional web references, Google Translate, model downloads and public AI reports involve user-initiated transfers to the listed third parties; do not use those features if you do not want the transfer. Privacy inquiries may be sent to the support email on the Google Play listing; the developer will designate a privacy contact for the listing before release.

Japan

Under the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI), you may request disclosure, correction, suspension of use, or deletion of retained personal data, and may contact the developer with complaints. Use the support email on the Google Play listing. Optional transfers to third-party providers occur only when you start the relevant feature.

AI-generated content

Reading AI output can be inaccurate. The app provides an in-app report/flag path for AI answers and generated artifacts, and requires confirmation that the report is public before submission. Do not submit personal information, confidential documents or sensitive data in a report.
